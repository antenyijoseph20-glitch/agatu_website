# Sector 02 — Natural Resources, Environment and Investment Intelligence

**Status:** Working research baseline; pending review.

## 1. Purpose

Document natural assets, environmental conditions and responsible investment opportunities without overstating geological evidence, resource reserves, commercial viability or land rights.

## 2. Geographic and environmental context

The 2019 UN Women/Institute for Peace and Conflict Resolution baseline describes Agatu's Benue River setting, fadama/irrigable land, highlands and western forest areas. Historical studies and flood reports identify riverine and agricultural exposure. These are contextual sources, not a current environmental survey.

A 2006 shallow-aquifer study described an approximately 25,000-hectare floodplain, alluvial deposits and 15 exploratory wells by BNARDA, with historical reported yields of 1.5–5 L/s. It does not establish present well function, current water quality or irrigation feasibility.

## 3. Mineral and salt/brine leads

- Benue State Ministry mineral portal associates Agatu with salt/brine springs or saliferous formations in the Benue Trough. It does not provide verified Agatu coordinates, quantified reserves, grade or commercial viability.
- NGSA National Mineral Occurrence Database and salt/brine study are authoritative research starting points. The accessible report describes 21 samples but names locations including Akiri (Nasarawa) and Shagu (Gombe); the reviewed material did not establish Agatu samples.
- A 2004 study of traditional salt extraction discusses locations in the wider Benue Trough, including Olachor–Abachor and Awe–Azara–Keana; it does not establish all sites as Agatu. The study raises questions about modern plant feasibility and the place of traditional producers.

Required distinction:
1. Community/resource claim.
2. Geological indication.
3. Field-sampled occurrence.
4. Quantified deposit/reserve.
5. Technical/economic feasibility.
6. Licensed exploration or operation.

Do not advertise mineral reserves or commercial viability without competent geological evidence and lawful permits.

## 4. Natural-resource register requirements

Capture resource type, claim/description, exact source, evidence class, coordinates and accuracy, survey/sample date, custodian/community, land-access status, environmental sensitivity, licence/permit status, verification, uncertainty, safety/publication restrictions and independent technical review.

## 5. Investment leads

- NIPC Benue investment profile says BIPC has designated agricultural investment zones in Apa, Agatu and Kwande. Exact Agatu location, legal status, land availability, infrastructure and investor status need primary evidence.
- 2026 AgroBenue $530m integrated agro-industrial MoU is statewide; no Agatu site established.
- 2026 Benue-AgriPro ₦25bn partnership initially identifies Mbatiev, Buruku, not Agatu.
- Benue 2025 Business-Enabling Reform Action Plan proposes integrated agro-industrial parks across 23 LGAs; implementation status and Agatu site require confirmation.
- 2022 report describes rice milling machines donated to women in Obagaji by Development Communication Incorporated (formerly ARDA), with Canada Fund for Local Initiatives support and training planned. Number, capacity, site, beneficiary records and current operation require inspection.

## 6. Environment and hazards

Flood events reported in 2017, 2019, 2022 and October 2026 affected Agatu communities, farms, schools, homes, water sources and livelihoods. October 2026 reporting described a rapid assessment by Benue SEMA, IOM, UNHCR and Red Cross and referred to 32 communities; obtain the actual assessment. NEMA 2022 flood needs assessment and 2020/2022 relief records are leads.

A 2026 draft budget includes an Agatu erosion/flood-control entry (code 20100125000200; location code 10730200), with 2025 revised budget ₦284,484,769.36, Jan–Sep performance shown as dash, and 2026 executive proposal ₦84,484,770.00. These are different budget columns; proposal is not approval or execution.

Benue State Development Plan 2025–2034 proposes multi-LGA drainage/flood control and riverbank tree planting; Agatu is named but the programme total is not Agatu-only.

## 7. Requirements

- Environmental asset register: rivers, wetlands, forests, floodplains, erosion sites, biodiversity and community-managed resources.
- Hazard register: flood, erosion, drought, pollution and other evidenced risks.
- Investment opportunity register: evidence, legal status, infrastructure, environmental/social safeguards and community consultation.
- Field verification: dated inspection, verifier, location accuracy, photos, observed condition, limitations and review.
- Public information must avoid sensitive ecological, security or culturally restricted locations where disclosure creates harm.
- Environmental and social impact, land access, community consent and statutory permits must be assessed before investor promotion.

## 8. Research gaps

Obtain NGSA site-specific evidence; verify BIPC zone location and legal status; inspect rice-processing equipment; obtain flood assessment reports and hydrological data; establish current resource use and environmental condition; verify investment MoUs and project implementation.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

No unverified resource is marketed as a proven reserve. Statewide programmes are not attributed to Agatu without evidence. All environmental and investment claims carry source, date, uncertainty and legal/safeguard status.
