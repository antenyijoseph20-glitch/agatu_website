# Sector 14 — Industry, Minerals and Natural-Resource Investment

**Status:** Working research baseline; pending geological, legal and investment verification.

## 1. Mineral/salt leads

- Benue State Ministry mineral portal associates Agatu with salt/brine springs or saliferous formations in the Benue Trough; no exact Agatu site, reserve, grade or commercial viability is established.
- NGSA National Mineral Occurrence Database and salt/brine report are official starting points. Reviewed material describes 21 samples but names Akiri (Nasarawa) and Shagu (Gombe); it does not establish Agatu samples.
- 2004 traditional salt-extraction study discusses wider Benue Trough sites including Olachor–Abachor and Awe–Azara–Keana; do not assume all are in Agatu. Study raises modern plant feasibility and local producer inclusion concerns.

## 2. Investment and industry leads

- NIPC Benue profile says BIPC has designated agricultural investment zones in Apa, Agatu and Kwande; exact Agatu site, legal status, land, infrastructure and investor activity need primary evidence.
- 2026 AgroBenue $530m integrated agro-industrial MoU is statewide; no Agatu site established.
- 2026 Benue-AgriPro ₦25bn partnership initially identifies Mbatiev, Buruku, not Agatu.
- Benue 2025 Business-Enabling Reform Action Plan proposes integrated agro-industrial parks across 23 LGAs; implementation and Agatu location require confirmation.
- 2022 report describes rice milling machines donated to women in Obagaji by Development Communication Incorporated (formerly ARDA), with Canada Fund for Local Initiatives support and training planned; verify equipment, beneficiary group and operation.

## 3. Mining title distinctions

Check Nigeria Mining Cadastre Office records and relevant permit categories: reconnaissance permit, exploration licence, small-scale mining lease, mining lease, quarry lease and water-use permit as applicable. A mineral occurrence does not prove a valid title or active licensed operation.

## 4. Requirements

Investment opportunity record: resource/evidence class, geology, permits, land-access status, infrastructure, environmental/social risks, community consultation, feasibility, project sponsor, funding status, implementation, jobs and local benefit evidence.

## 5. Safeguards

Do not market unverified mineral deposits or imply title/ownership. No land allocation, resource certification or investor guarantee by the platform. Require competent technical assessment, statutory permits, environmental/social assessment and community engagement.

## 6. Research gaps

Obtain NGSA site-specific reports, mining cadastre extracts, BIPC zone documents, investor agreements, feasibility studies, environmental permits, equipment inspection and actual local-benefit evidence.
