# Sector 12 — Security, Displacement and Humanitarian Recovery

**Status:** Working research baseline; sensitive sector requiring restricted-by-default design.

## 1. Evidence leads

- IOM DTM Aug 1 2025: conflict displacement affecting Okutenobe in Ogwule Ogbaulu Ward; report recorded 133 affected and four fatalities. Historical event; do not expose operationally sensitive details.
- IOM June 2025 reports incidents including Eguma; one flash report recorded 49 displaced people from Eguma to Ogwule-Kaduna.
- Another July–Aug 2025 report recorded 96 displaced from Okutenobe and four fatalities. Some reports may overlap in time/population; do not sum without deduplication.
- IOM DTM Round 18 IDP and Returnee Atlas, Feb 2026, estimates Agatu at 27,993 IDPs and 17,510 returnees based on Sep–Oct 2025 assessment. These are estimates, not live figures or individual registers. Return does not necessarily mean durable or safe.
- IOM biometric registration in 2024 recorded 21,861 IDPs across three Agatu host-community sites and 5,641 households; registration scope/method differs from DTM estimates. Do not add or compare as if identical measures.
- NEMA 2016 additional relief for Agatu IDPs; media reports on state relief at an IDP camp in Apa; post-attack reconstruction needs in 2016. Obtain primary distribution records and beneficiary verification.
- Benue SEMA 2025 intention survey: 911 households across 18 sites in five LGAs; 95% wanted return, 89% reported housing unavailable/access barriers, 10% fishing as accessible livelihood. Agatu was 2% of intended-return destinations in the survey, not 2% of Agatu residents.
- Benue SEMA 2025 socioeconomic assessment and Durable Solutions Action Plan Jan 2025 are relevant state-level sources.
- ICPC announced tracking 34 federal constituency projects in 14 Benue LGAs including Agatu in Feb 2026; actual Agatu findings not obtained.

## 2. Separate registers

1. Security/displacement events.
2. Humanitarian assistance and relief distributions.
3. Reconstruction and rehabilitation.
4. Resettlement and durable solutions.
5. Institutional response and referrals.
6. Community recovery and livelihood support.

Each event/assessment must retain date, geographic scope, methodology, population definition, reporting institution, uncertainty, source and revision history.

## 3. Requirements and safeguards

- Restricted incident-reporting workflow.
- Anonymous/confidential reporting where appropriate.
- Safe referral to authorised institutions.
- Aggregate public reporting only where disclosure is safe.
- Separate displaced persons, host communities, returnees and people in transit.
- Do not assume return is safe, voluntary or durable.
- Protect names, informants, shelter locations, routes, security movements and operational details.
- Do not publish allegations as established facts.
- Do not create unauthorised surveillance, enforcement or live tactical maps.

## 4. Research gaps

Obtain primary DTM datasets/reports, SEMA assessments, NEMA records, humanitarian agency project reports, safe community consultation and institutional referral protocols. Reconcile overlap between registration, event snapshots and estimates.

## 5. Acceptance criteria

Every number identifies date, scope, method and population definition. Sensitive records are restricted. Public data is safely aggregated. Event reports are not added together without deduplication.
