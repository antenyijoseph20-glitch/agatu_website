# Sector 16 — Telecommunications and Digital Infrastructure

**Status:** Working research baseline; current ward-level service quality remains unverified.

## 1. Network coverage and quality

NCC dataset search results include Agatu LGA network-performance indicators, including Airtel 2G/3G availability and other service-quality measures. Extracted table context and reporting periods need confirmation before using values.

NCC subscriber-compensation register lists Agatu for Airtel in December 2025 and January 2026, indicating performance below required quality-of-service thresholds for the relevant area/time. This does not mean no coverage or that every subscriber was affected. Absence from another operator's compensation list does not prove good coverage.

NCC publishes monthly LGA-level radio-access network indicators for Airtel, MTN, Globacom and 9mobile. Compare operator data with actual ward-level tests and resident experience.

## 2. Infrastructure programmes

- USPF published subsidised base-station register lists Ofoke in Apa, Ugboko Edikwu Icho in Apa and Agila in Ado, but not Agatu. This does not prove Agatu has no towers.
- USPF Rural Broadband Initiative register includes an Otukpo Benue broadband project in 2018; does not prove extension into Agatu.
- Federal Project BRIDGE aims for at least 90,000km fibre and connectivity to more than 770 LGAs; 2026 investor prequalification confirms national programme, not an Agatu route or delivery schedule.
- Benue State Development Plan 2025–2034 identifies inadequate electricity, computer systems and internet facilities, weak institutional connectivity and underdeveloped e-governance. Statewide targets include internet penetration 80%, broadband 50%, basic digital literacy 50%, secure ICT infrastructure 90% and 90% online government services by 2029. These are targets, not achieved Agatu outcomes.

## 3. Public ICT and skills

Agatu Council 2026 tender includes a 250-seat ICT centre at Pr. Alla Skill Acquisition Centre, Obagaji. Tender only; not evidence of award, construction, equipment, connectivity or operation.

Benue plan reports statewide ICT training for 10,000 youths during 2020–2024 and digital hubs/computer-based centres; Agatu beneficiaries and current facility operation are unverified.

## 4. Requirements

- Ward/settlement coverage and service-quality register.
- Operators: Airtel, MTN, Glo, 9mobile and any other available providers.
- Mobile data, fixed wireless, fibre, satellite and other technologies.
- Public ICT centres, school labs, digital hubs and cybercafés.
- Digital literacy programmes, providers, beneficiaries and outcomes.
- Government online services and access barriers.
- Community network reports with date, provider, technology, device/test method, result and confidence.
- Offline field collection, delayed sync, low-bandwidth pages, assisted submission and accessible mobile layouts.

Distinguish tower presence from operational coverage and service quality. Do not publish sensitive tower security, power backup or vulnerabilities.

## 5. Data protection

Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 requirements include lawful and transparent processing, data minimisation, retention limitation, accuracy, security, data-subject rights, breach response and DPIA/registration obligations where applicable. Assess the platform's actual role and obligations with appropriate advice.

## 6. Research gaps

Obtain NCC underlying datasets and reporting definitions; operator coverage/complaint data; ward field tests; fibre routes; USPF records; ICT-centre status; school connectivity; public Wi-Fi; historical telecom arrival and rollout.

## 7. Acceptance criteria

No ward is labelled covered based only on a tower point. Each measurement is dated and method-labelled. Platform remains usable on weak connections and can collect field data offline.
