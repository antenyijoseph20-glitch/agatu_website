# Sector 19 — Population, Demographics, Migration and Social Development

**Status:** Working research baseline; current population and ward estimates remain incomplete.

## 1. 2006 census baseline

NBS Annual Abstract of Statistics 2011, using NPC figures, records Agatu's 2006 population as 115,597:
- Male 58,478.
- Female 57,119.

This is a historical census count, not the 2026 population. NPC FAQ indicates Nigeria's last conducted census was 2006; 2025 announcement about preparations did not provide a new census result.

## 3. Historical projections

NBS series lists Agatu projections:
- 2008: 122,745.
- 2009: 126,483.
- 2010: 130,335.

These are historical projections, not census counts. Statewide Benue projections are not Agatu-specific. Do not publish a new 2026 projection without explicit methodology, assumptions, uncertainty and treatment of displacement/return.

## 4. Literacy

NBS National Literacy Survey 2010 Agatu entry:
- English literacy: male 66.5%, female 50.4%, both 59.1%.
- Literacy in any language: male 73.4%, female 58.4%, both 66.5%.

Historical only, not current rates.

## 5. Displacement and return

- IOM biometric registration in 2024 recorded 21,861 IDPs across three Agatu host-community sites and 5,641 households. Registration scope and method differ from DTM estimates.
- IOM June 2025 flash report recorded 49 displaced from Eguma to Ogwule-Kaduna.
- July–Aug 2025 report recorded 96 displaced from Okutenobe and four fatalities; reports may overlap with other snapshots.
- IOM DTM Round 18, Feb 2026, estimates 27,993 IDPs and 17,510 returnees in Agatu based on Sep–Oct 2025 assessment. Estimates, not live counts or individual registers.
- Benue SEMA 2025 intention survey: 911 households, 18 sites, five LGAs; 95% wanted return; 89% said housing unavailable/access barrier; 10% identified fishing as accessible livelihood. Agatu represented 2% of survey intended-return destinations, not 2% of Agatu population.
- Benue SEMA 2025 socioeconomic assessment and Durable Solutions Action Plan Jan 2025 are relevant.

Do not add different registration and assessment totals without harmonising reference dates, definitions, scope and deduplication.

## 6. Poverty and livelihoods

NBS 2022 MPI reports Benue statewide multidimensional poverty incidence of 75.0%; not Agatu-specific.

2024 academic study on communal crisis and food security in Agatu reports sample of 381 respondents and farming as main occupation among respondents. Sample study, not complete population/employment register.

Investigate water, electricity, housing, sanitation, school attendance, health access, employment, agricultural income, food security, displacement and household resilience.

## 7. Requirements

Six components:
1. Population statistics.
2. Ward/settlement demographics.
3. Migration/displacement.
4. Education/literacy indicators.
5. Livelihoods/poverty indicators.
6. Social inclusion/vulnerability.

Each statistic: definition, geographic area, reference date, value/unit, sample/population, method, institution, source/page, verification, uncertainty and revision history. Prominently display data year.

## 8. Privacy

Do not publish names or household-level vulnerability/IDP data. Use safe aggregates and protect identity, shelter locations and sensitive status.

## 9. Research gaps

Obtain recent population estimates, ward-level primary data, current literacy, employment, household services, migration/return and vulnerability surveys with appropriate sampling and ethical review.
