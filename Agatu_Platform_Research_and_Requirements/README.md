## Important limitations
- Source reference IDs included in the documents are identifiers from the research conversation. They are not permanent public URLs. They must be resolved to exact source titles, institutions, dates and direct URLs in a source-register hardening pass.
- Some findings are secondary reports, programme announcements, draft budgets or historical records. Their verification limitations are stated in the relevant sector document.
- The library is based on the research notes available at consolidation time. It should be reviewed against the full prior conversation and original source documents before being declared complete.
- Do not code, modify the existing Agatu website or data, push to GitHub or deploy until the user has reviewed and explicitly approved the requirements.
