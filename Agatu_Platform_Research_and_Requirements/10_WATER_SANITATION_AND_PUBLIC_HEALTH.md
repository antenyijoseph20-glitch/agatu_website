## 3. Sanitation

Research toilets, household sanitation, public toilets, waste disposal, drainage, open-defecation validation, hygiene programmes, wastewater and environmental health services.

## 4. Requirements

Separate water source, water treatment, distribution, sanitation and hygiene assets. Track service availability and quality, not merely existence. Water-quality results must identify test date, method, laboratory and parameters.

## 5. Safety and privacy

Do not expose private household water access records or sensitive infrastructure vulnerabilities. Use aggregate service statistics.

## 6. Research gaps

## 7. Acceptance criteria

Every water asset has a dated functional inspection. A borehole listed in a budget is not described as operational. Water quality is not assumed from appearance or flow.
