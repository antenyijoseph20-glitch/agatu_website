# Sector 13 — Flooding, Climate and Disaster Management

**Status:** Working research baseline; safety and environmental review required.

## 1. Historical flood and disaster leads

Reports describe Agatu flood events in:
- 2017: more than 20 villages reportedly affected, including Ayele, Utugolugu, Okokolo, Obagaji, Enungba and Oshigbudu; farms and water sources affected.
- 2019: 37 Agatu communities reportedly affected; farms, schools, electricity and households affected.
- 2022: flood impacts documented in NEMA and other reports.
- October 2026: reporting describes Benue SEMA/IOM/UNHCR/Red Cross rapid assessment and 32 communities; obtain underlying assessment and exact scope.

NEMA 2022 flood needs assessment for Agatu and 2020/2022 relief reports are primary-source leads. Statewide figures must not be attributed to Agatu.

## 3. Requirements

Hazard register: event type, date/time, location, extent, source, severity, affected population/households, facilities, roads, farms, livelihood impacts, response, relief, recovery and uncertainty.

Physical inspection fields: flood exposure, drainage, riverbank protection, erosion, bridge/road damage, public facility damage, livelihoods, early warning, date, verifier, safe GPS, evidence and recommended mitigation.

## 4. Safety and data quality

Do not publish sensitive shelter locations, vulnerable household identities or details that increase exposure. Distinguish observed flood extent from modelled hazard and historical event reports. Do not treat one flood event as a permanent risk boundary.

## 5. Research gaps

Obtain official assessments, rainfall/hydrology records, flood maps, early-warning arrangements, drainage/erosion projects, resettlement records and current community risk observations.

## 6. Acceptance criteria

Every hazard record identifies event date, evidence type, geographic precision and affected population methodology. Budget entries are separate from physical mitigation outcomes.
