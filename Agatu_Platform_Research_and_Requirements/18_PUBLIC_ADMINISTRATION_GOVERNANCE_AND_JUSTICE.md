# Sector 18 — Public Administration, Traditional Governance, Justice and Institutional Services

**Status:** Working research baseline; authority and current officeholders require confirmation.

## 1. Agatu Local Government Council

Benue State directory identifies Agatu as one of 23 LGAs, headquarters Obagaji, and lists an Executive Chairman. A 7 Oct 2024 report records appointment of Peter Illa Idoko as Council Secretary and Yusuf Aduh as Chief Protocol Officer. These are dated records; confirm current officeholders and subsequent changes.

No complete current Agatu Council organisational chart and departmental directory was established.

Investigate Administration/General Services, Finance/Treasury, Works/Housing, Health/Social Services, Education, Agriculture/Natural Resources, Environment/Sanitation, Planning/Statistics, Personnel/Establishment, Legal and Procurement. These are departments to verify, not a claim every exact title currently exists.

## 2. Benue State Bureau of Local Government and Chieftaincy Affairs

Published functions include monitoring/supervision of 23 councils, evaluating council performance, traditional ruler recognition/appointments, local-government financial accountability and due process, contract monitoring, coordination with Local Government Service Commission, Auditor-General for Local Governments and SUBEB, administrative reform, ICT and community development.

Published structure includes Planning/Research/Statistics, Chieftaincy Affairs, Administration/Supply, Finance/Accounts, Local Government Affairs, Legal Services and Monitoring/Inspection.

Potential institutional contact for records and clarification; no automatic partnership or unrestricted access should be assumed.

## 3. Traditional governance

Benue describes three tiers: LGA Traditional Councils, Area Traditional Councils and State Council of Chiefs; Idoma and Tiv Area Traditional Councils are identified at state level. Confirm Agatu's recognised local structure, traditional rulers, district heads, community heads, recognition instruments and succession records.

A 2025 report describes a dispute over authority to confer traditional titles in Idoma institutions. Platform must not recognise rulers, confer titles or decide chieftaincy disputes. Preserve disputed claims with sources and status.

## 4. Courts and justice

Official judiciary directory lists:
- Customary Magistrate Court — Obagaji.
- Upper Area Court — Obagaji.
- Grade I Area Court — Ogwule Kaduna.
- Grade I Area Court — Okokolo Agatu.

Directory listing is not proof each court currently operates. Confirm location, jurisdiction, sitting days, contact and status with judiciary.

Benue Judiciary describes High Court, Magistracy, Area Courts and Customary Court of Appeal, with online services including e-filing, e-affidavits, payments and document verification.

## 5. Public safety institutions

Research current public directory for Police, NSCDC, FRSC, fire/emergency services, NEMA, Benue SEMA and health emergency/referral services. Agatu-specific station addresses, contacts and coverage remain unverified. Never guess emergency numbers.

Do not publish deployments, patrol schedules, vulnerabilities, informants or operational details.

## 6. Procurement and accountability

Benue State Public Procurement Commission portal publishes procurement opportunities, contractor registration, procurement documents and contract-related information. Its described regulatory remit includes state MDAs and local councils.

Benue State Public Procurement Law 2020 and 2024 Amendment are listed by Commission. Obtain current legal texts and verify applicability to each entity/project.

Benue State Fiscal Responsibility Law 2020 requires quarterly budget-execution summaries within 30 days after quarter end and consolidated reports within six months after financial year, as described in the published law. Obtain original official law and determine enforcement/current compliance.

Public Complaints Commission Benue describes mandate to investigate maladministration complaints involving government and certain private organisations, with alternative dispute resolution/remedies.

## 7. Requirements

Institution register: legal name, mandate, authority, service area, office location, public contact, office status, source, date and verification.

Traditional institution register: recognition instrument, jurisdiction, public functions, custodian, source and dispute status.

Justice directory: court category, confirmed jurisdiction, address, operating status, public contact and accessible services.

Accountability register: budget documents, procurement, awards, public complaints/referrals, institutional response and status.

## 8. Boundaries and safeguards

The platform is not a government system, court, police service, procurement regulator, land authority or traditional council. It cannot adjudicate disputes or compel agencies to act. Public submissions remain observations until reviewed. Do not publish confidential case information or allegations as findings.
