# Sector 03 — Agatu LGA Historical Development Projects Inventory, 1991–2026

**Status:** Working discovery inventory and requirements baseline; incomplete; not approved for implementation.  
**Coverage:** Federal, Benue State, Agatu Local Government, development partners and other institutions. Keep institutional attribution distinct.

## 1. Objective and coverage

Build a comprehensive, evidence-based history of development projects across all ten Agatu wards from LGA creation in 1991 through 24 November 2026. Include proposed, budgeted, approved, awarded, ongoing, delayed, abandoned, rehabilitated, completed, cancelled and unknown projects.

Do not claim completeness until research covers all relevant years, wards, institutions, archives and source classes.

## 2. Mandatory preservation rule

Never delete a discovered budget entry or project because it was not implemented, is incomplete, abandoned, delayed, disputed or of unknown status. Preserve all versions and source evidence. A record may gain stronger verification status as evidence improves, but its history must remain.

## 3. Required project fields

Project name/description; preliminary ID; sector; community/ward; location/coordinates/accuracy; funding institution; implementing agency; budget reference; proposed/budgeted/approved/contracted/released/paid amounts; contractor; procurement method/tender; award date; contract reference; planned/actual start and completion; status; physical progress; defects/delay reasons; handover/commissioning; operational condition; source documents; photos; last verification; submitter; reviewer; review date/decision/reason; audit history.

Model parent project and separate funding packages/contracts where multiple institutions fund different components. Avoid double-counting overlapping road corridors or co-funded projects.

## 4. Verification stages

1. Discovery and source catalogue.
2. Document verification.
3. Contract/procurement verification.
4. Physical site verification.
5. Independent corroboration.
6. Publication decision.

A budget document verifies a budget entry, not an award or delivery. An award notice does not prove commencement. Payment does not prove completion. Photos alone are insufficient. Operational status requires a dated functional inspection.

## 15. Acceptance criteria

Every discovered project/budget entry is preserved. Financial stages are separate. Overlapping corridors are not double-counted. Each status is supported by claim-specific evidence. No project is called completed or operational without adequate dated evidence. All ten wards and all levels of government/institutions are covered.
