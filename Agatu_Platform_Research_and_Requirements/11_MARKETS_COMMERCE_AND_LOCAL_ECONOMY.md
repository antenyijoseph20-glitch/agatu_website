## 2. Commerce and enterprise

Research traditional markets, traders' associations, abattoirs, transport facilities, storage, market access, local manufacturers, artisans, agricultural traders, business registration, women/youth enterprises and employment.

Distinguish a market location from a publicly funded market construction project. A public directory listing does not establish licensing or regulatory compliance.

## 3. Requirements

Market register: name, aliases, ward/community, location, market days, commodities, ownership/management, facilities, sanitation, accessibility, transport links, safety, current operation and source.

Business register: voluntary business profile, category, contact/publication consent, location, operating status, CAC status only if verified, applicable licence status only if verified, products/services and update date.

Project links should use the master project register and preserve financial/verification distinctions.

## 4. Research gaps

Verify market locations and condition; market history and days; bus/transport services; abattoirs; storage/cold chain; trader groups; market rehabilitation; formal/informal business counts; local employment and SME support.

## 5. Acceptance criteria

No business is described as registered/licensed without authoritative evidence. Tender and budget entries are not represented as completed market facilities.
