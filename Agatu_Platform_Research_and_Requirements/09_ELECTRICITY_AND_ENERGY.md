## 2. Requirements

For each asset/corridor: type, capacity, location, ward, provider/owner, implementing agency, contractor, project/financial history, connection status, energisation date, hours/reliability, households/businesses served where measured, outage pattern, safety, maintenance, inspection and evidence.

Distinguish:
- Transformer delivered vs installed vs energised vs functional.
- Poles/lines constructed vs connected supply.
- Solar equipment installed vs batteries/inverter/panels functional.
- Public lighting installed vs currently operating.

## 3. Safety

Do not publish sensitive infrastructure vulnerabilities, detailed security arrangements or exploitable site information. Public maps should use appropriate generalisation.

## 4. Research gaps

Inspect all ten wards; obtain PHCN/DisCo and REA records; verify transformer and line assets; establish supply reliability, public facility solar installations, mini-grid/standalone systems and maintenance responsibilities.

## 5. Acceptance criteria

Each energy asset has a dated operational status and source. Project funding and physical energisation are separate. No current reliability claim is based solely on a project announcement.
