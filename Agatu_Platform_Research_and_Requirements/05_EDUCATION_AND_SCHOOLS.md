## 3. Requirements

School directory fields:
- Official name and alternatives; school code if available.
- Ward/community/address and coordinates with accuracy.
- School type, ownership/management, education level.
- Enrolment by year and source; do not expose identifiable pupils.
- Classrooms, toilets, water, electricity, accessibility, ICT and learning facilities.
- Teacher numbers/qualification aggregates where authorised.
- Current operational status and last inspection.
- Funding/project history linked to project register.
- Source, verification status and change history.

Education project records must use the master project fields and physical verification workflow.

## 4. Safety and privacy

No public pupil names, individual attendance, disability/health details or personal staff information. Use aggregate statistics. Obtain consent for identifiable photographs and interviews.
