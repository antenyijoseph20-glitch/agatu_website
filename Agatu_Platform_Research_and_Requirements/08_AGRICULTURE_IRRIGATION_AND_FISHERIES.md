# Sector 08 — Agriculture, Irrigation and Fisheries

**Status:** Working research baseline; pending review.

## 1. Economic context

Agatu is described in regional and academic sources as an agricultural and fishing LGA. A 2019 UN Women/IPCR baseline notes riverine/fadama land, farming and fishing. A 2019 Federal University of Agriculture Makurdi study sampled 125 rice farmers across five Agatu wards. These sources do not constitute a current census of farmers or production.

## 2. Irrigation and water infrastructure

- LBRBDA Obagaji Irrigation Project page describes 1,000ha potential, farmland cleared, water lifted from dam, office/residential buildings, and a plan for 1,000ha gravity plus 60ha pivot irrigation. Current operation and cultivated area unknown.
- Federal Ministry of Water Resources 2020 dams compendium records Obagaji Dam completed but silted; historical condition only.
- ERGP5157638: 50ha irrigable farmland clearing at Obagaji, ₦20m proposed in 2022 executive proposal.
- ERGP28157643: Obagaji Dam/irrigation rehabilitation ₦37,593,714 in 2022 appropriation amendment.

Do not equate dam construction, irrigation potential, farmland clearing, water lifting and current functioning irrigation service.

## 3. IFAD/VCDP and rice production

Secondary academic paper reproducing IFAD Benue Office 2024 data reports 686 Agatu rice beneficiaries and 47 farmer organisations across 2019–2023:
- 2019: 19 organisations, 410 beneficiaries.
- 2020: 22 organisations, 195 beneficiaries.
- 2021: 3 organisations, 53 beneficiaries.
- 2022: 1 organisation, 11 beneficiaries.
- 2023: 2 organisations, 17 beneficiaries.

Obtain primary beneficiary/organisation registers and programme records. Avoid double-counting repeat beneficiaries across years.

## 4. Processing, storage and aggregation project leads

- Rice aggregation centre, Obagaji: 2023 budget ₦3.5m; 2024 revised ₦35m; no expenditure recorded in relevant table.
- Agatu Rice Mill electrification: 2024 revised ₦6m, table interpretation inconsistent; 2026 proposed ₦5,813,817.23.
- 2026 proposal: cassava processing centres at Usha and Warri (amount not established in extracted record).
- 2026 proposal: two rice processing centres/parboiling sheds/drying slabs at Usha and Oshigbudu, ₦76,548,593.56.
- 2026 proposal: aggregation centre Oweto, ₦58,138,172.32.
- 2022 report: rice milling machines donated to women in Obagaji by Development Communication Incorporated, formerly ARDA, with Canada Fund for Local Initiatives support; number, capacity, beneficiary group, training and current operation unknown.

## 5. Fisheries, livestock and other agricultural sectors

Historical sources describe fishing and use of canoes/boats along the Benue. Further research is needed on:
- Fish landing sites, ponds, fishing groups and processing.
- Fishing seasons, gear, cold storage and market chains.
- Livestock/poultry, veterinary and animal-health services.
- Cassava, yam, rice, beans and other crop production.
- Extension services, input distribution, mechanisation and farmer grants.
- Post-harvest loss, storage, transport and market access.
- Irrigation water quality and environmental sustainability.

## 6. Requirements

Farmer/cooperative register; crop and fisheries profile; agricultural facility register; irrigation asset register; processing/storage register; programme/beneficiary register with privacy protections; seasonal production indicators with source/method; project linkage and physical inspection.

Do not publish individual beneficiary details or claim programme benefits without source verification. Keep programme targets, allocations, actual disbursements and outcomes separate.

## 7. Acceptance criteria

All agricultural claims include location, period, source and method. Irrigation and processing assets have dated operational inspections. Beneficiary figures are deduplicated and privacy-protected.
